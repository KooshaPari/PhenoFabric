## Product conclusion

Ableton-class audio is not a side channel of remote desktop. It is an independently scheduled graph with explicit clock domains, buffers, xrun policy, admission control and fallback. Remote DSP is allowed only when the measured end-to-end deadline—including both network directions and clock adaptation—fits the graph.
