# Research Program

## Streams

### RS-1 — Locality and stage elimination

Characterize the real cost of copies, memory-domain transitions, codecs and synchronization on same-process, same-OS, host↔VM and PCIe paths. Deliver a calibrated route cost library.

### RS-2 — Interactive media and professional audio

Measure input-to-photon, frame pacing, HDR/color transforms, audio clocks, ASRC, xruns and mixed-load behavior. Deliver admission/degradation policies tied to actual endpoints.

### RS-3 — Compute and object placement

Build explicit TaskSpec/object baselines, then evaluate adaptive fusion/fission, prefetch, speculation and selected interposition. Deliver a bounded empirical granularity map.

### RS-4 — Seamless surfaces

Compare semantic protocols with pixel proxies, characterize owned-window graphs, IME/DPI/secure surfaces and stream atlas economics. Deliver per-app route policy and compatibility corpus.

### RS-5 — Security and failure

Test pairing, capabilities, privilege separation, leases, partitions, adapter compromise, OOB and recovery. Deliver threat evidence and safe fallback contracts.

### RS-6 — Product usability

Test whether one shell actually reduces workflow cost, and whether explanations/degradation are actionable. Deliver workspace templates and expert/simple interaction rules.

## Review cadence

Every research item has an owner, next decision date, active hypothesis, experiment ID, evidence URI and one of: `proposed`, `running`, `supported`, `narrowed`, `rejected`, `graduated`. No item remains “interesting” indefinitely.
